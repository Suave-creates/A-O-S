Locked Totes

What is locked in the ASRS right now - by priority, age and aisle

What this screen does

Read-only. Fetches the Locked Tote Dump (Grafana), Supervisor Dump and Quadron Inventory, classifies every locked tote as SD / P0 / P1 / P2 and tracks how long each has been locked. It never pulls a tote, creates a pull session or enters the Quadron pull queue - it only answers "what is the current ASRS situation?" so you can decide what to do. Quadron decides what is locked: a tote from the Locked Tote Dump is counted only when Quadron's inventory holds it with stock; the others are listed apart under Not in Quadron and never counted. If Quadron cannot be read, the refresh keeps the last snapshot. SD = on the Supervisor dump; P0 = the only tote in the ASRS holding one of its PIDs, P1 = 2-3 totes, P2 = 4 or more. Click a tile, a grid cell or an aisle to filter the list.

Locked Tote Analytics

Reported, counted, ghost and repeat totes - how cycle counts really happen

How totes are classified

Report - a SHORT discrepancy on a tote at a picking station; it locks the tote and asks for a cycle count. Proper CC - a GTP stock check of a tote reported in the 3 days before it (one report counts for one check). Ghost tote - a stock check with no report before it: the tote was called manually - through the AOS Manual or Auto Puller, or outside AOS (Quadron / GTP directly). Purged - counted inside a Manual Puller purge; never a ghost.

Repeat tote - the same tote counted again within 48 hours. Repeat SKU - another tote counted within 48 hours holding a SKU that came up SHORT in a reported tote's count. Each repeat keeps its own class, so you see whether it was reported or called manually. Outcome of a count: All ALRIGHT matched the system (a false report or a needless call), Short / Excess less / more than the system, Bermuda nothing found. Days run 06:00 to 06:00 IST. Read-only.

ASRS Performance

The One Pager kept day by day - month to date and daily averages

 
What this screen does

Every morning at 06:05 IST the server saves the One Pager for the day before - its Shift C has closed by then, so production, cycle count and compaction are final; locked totes, outside totes, utilisation and aisle occupancy are recorded as they stand at that moment. The month to date (MTD) totals and the daily averages here move as each day is added.

Days the server missed are filled in by themselves (or with Fill missing days). For those, production, cycle count and compaction are rebuilt for that date; locked, outside and utilisation figures exist only if someone generated a One Pager for that date at the time - otherwise they are blank, never zero. Read-only: nothing here changes any operation.

One Pager Report

The daily ASRS snapshot, or any range of days, ready to share

What this screen does

Daily report: Locked Totes, Compaction, CC Done / Discrepancies / Station Coverage, Totes In ASRS, Tote Utilisation, Outside Totes, ASRS Production and Aisle Occupancy - every section fetched fresh on Generate, never dependent on another screen having refreshed. Compaction, CC, Station Coverage and ASRS Production use the Report date for their A/B/C shift windows; every other section is the live, current state whatever the date. Outside Totes counts only totes classified as Outside Tote (not Bad Tracker), split into 12–24 hours and over 24 hours.

Day-range report: ASRS Production, CC Metrics and Compaction / Tote Freed, one row per day with a TOTAL and AVERAGE row - for a 15-day (or any) review. Locked Totes, Utilisation, Outside Totes and Aisle Occupancy are right-now snapshots with no per-day history, so they are only in the daily report.
